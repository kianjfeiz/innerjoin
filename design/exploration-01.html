motion — the spec

the curvecubic-bezier(.2, 1.4, .3, 1) — everything arrives with momentum and a little overshoot. nothing eases linearly, ever.
the thunkblocks land first; the hard shadow snaps in ~60ms later. that one-frame delay is what makes depth feel like weight instead of decoration.
base tempo240–340ms per entrance, 90ms stagger between siblings — eager, never frantic.
thinking liveretrieval narrated in mono with a blinking block cursor. blue while working, dims when done. the app never hides behind a spinner.
birthnew categories pop in with a −2° twist and an orange flash before settling into their swatch color. rare, loud, earned.
respectprefers-reduced-motion jumps every sequence to its final frame.
